# Homepage Presentation Assessment

Reviewed on live production after the September 20, 2026 UTC cutover.

Live captures: [desktop](editorial-refresh/production-home-desktop.png) and [mobile](editorial-refresh/production-home-mobile.png).

## Verdict

The redesign is clean, elegant, and professional, but the opening is still more formal than personal. Typography alone does most of the visual work. The next improvement should show the person and the making earlier, rather than adding generic AI decoration.

## Findings

1. **The opening is entirely text.** The name has strong presence, but there is no portrait, real artifact, or interactive moment in the hero. This is equally apparent on desktop and mobile.
2. **The project graphics also contain a lot of text.** Four synthetic previews repeat labels and frameworks before the case-study summaries repeat related ideas. They are responsibly labeled, but feel like miniature presentation slides.
3. **Real work arrives late.** At the inspected desktop size, the first actual repository screenshot begins approximately 2,200px down the page. Main content contains approximately 452 words, including the preview labels. The issue is visual rhythm, not simply excessive word count.
4. **The voice establishes competence more than personality.** Production, operations, contracts, and frameworks dominate. Scientific curiosity, already supported by the biography, is a stronger personal entry point.
5. **The composition is uniformly restrained.** Symmetric project pairs, muted surfaces, fine rules, and repeated information rows are orderly but offer little surprise.

## Recommended Next Pass

Keep the name-led identity, elegant typography, clear navigation, qualified outcomes, and quiet case-study pages. Make the homepage feel like a curious engineer introducing their work.

| Priority | Adjustment | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bring one genuine workbench image or short user-controlled demo into the opening composition, large and unframed. | Show the making, not only the positioning. |
| 2 | Replace the two overlapping hero explanations with one short first-person introduction. | Make the entry warmer and easier to absorb. |
| 3 | Reduce project summaries to one sentence; preserve detail and qualifications in the case studies. | Create breathing room without weakening evidence. |
| 4 | Simplify thumbnail graphics into visual cause-and-effect compositions with less microcopy. | Let the graphics communicate instead of repeating text. |
| 5 | Move a short personal note higher, built around scientific curiosity and turning research into useful products. | Express personality supported by existing content. |
| 6 | Add one restrained playful interaction, with keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, and static fallbacks. | Introduce delight without distracting from the work. |

## Suggested Voice

Keep the H1 as **Shailesh Dudala.** A proposed supporting line:

> Curious about how things work. Serious about making them useful.

Follow with one factual sentence:

> I build AI systems for healthcare and insurance, and explore the ideas behind them in public.

These are copy proposals, not a new personal quotation or an unsupported biographical claim.

## Asset Boundaries

- Existing repository screenshots and recorded demos are appropriate assets.
- No approved personal portrait was found in the repository. The public GitHub avatar is an identicon. Do not invent or generate a likeness.
- A genuine portrait or workbench photograph could add more personality than an abstract illustration; this needs an owner-provided asset.
- Do not invent hobbies, anecdotes, production screenshots, employer details, or metrics.
- Preserve team credit and outcome qualifications.

## Acceptance

Meaningful visual content should be visible in the first desktop and mobile viewport. The opening should contain one clear idea and one primary action. Project previews should communicate without requiring small-label reading. Keep the existing responsive, accessibility, static-content, and reduced-motion guarantees.

The approved release was deployed unchanged. These recommendations were not silently mixed into its production cutover.
